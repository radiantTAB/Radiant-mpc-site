Design a Mold

Build a two-part mold for casting silicone

Upload a watertight bolus STL. The shell core/cavity engine molds it the way it sits on the patient — a core tray that replicates the anatomy and a cavity plug that lowers over it — so scalp, ear and other curved boluses release cleanly with no flat-plane undercuts. A built-in pour funnel and registration pegs are added automatically. Prints support-free in PLA or PETG.

5 Registration pegs

Print tip (shell mode): tray prints flat-bottom down, core dome up; plug prints upside-down resting on its flat plateau (add a brim) — no supports on either half; every plug slope is ≤ 45° by construction. Molds aren't dose-relevant: 2 walls + 10–15 % gyroid infill is plenty. PLA or PETG both work for room-temperature-cure silicone (use a mold release; avoid PLA for hot-pour materials). If the plug binds on the tray's silhouette wall, set slicer XY compensation to −0.1 mm on the plug.
