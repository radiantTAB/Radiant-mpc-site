Booking a meeting takes seconds

Three simple steps — for you and for the people booking with you.

Set your availability

Tell Meetly when you're free. Create event types like a 15-minute intro or a 60-minute deep dive.

Share your link

Drop your Meetly link in an email, text, or on your site. Invitees see only your open times.

Get booked

They pick a slot, it lands on both calendars, and everyone gets a confirmation. Done.
