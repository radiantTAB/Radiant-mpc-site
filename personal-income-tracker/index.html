Income Tracker

Hours and income per site per week. Each site's average weekly hours are carried out to a full-year projection at its rate — including mid-year rate changes — and compared against prior years.

Year summary
Per-site projection

Run-rate sites: avg weekly hours × weeks × rate (a mid-year rate change adds a second segment, e.g. 27 wk @ old + 52 wk @ new). Actual-hours sites: hours recorded so far × rate. Manual sites: a fixed hours figure × rate. Set each site's method in Sites & rates below.

Year over year — average weekly hours

Editable history — your recorded per-site figures by year. Click a cell to change it.

Total projection by year

Prior years are editable; the current year auto-fills from the projection above.

Weekly entry

Type hours for each site — dollars auto-fill from the current rate (editable). Entries save automatically. Clear both to remove a week for a site.

Sites & rates
Timeclock & pay periods

Each entry: date, clock in/out (hours auto-calc), and cost center GK or RO. Week 1 = the pay-period start week; week 2 = the following week. Totals split GK/RO per week, matching the KMC pay-period sheet.

RadOnc charts by day & Gamma Knife Co-Reg

RO charts are bucketed Sun/Mon · Tue · Wed · Thu · Fri/Sat (weekly total auto-sums). The footer shows season totals and per-day averages across the weeks recorded — like the "Kettering Charts" block on your sheet.
